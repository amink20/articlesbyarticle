What the app does

Financial Analyst turns an annual report into numbers you can check. The app does all the calculating. The AI only reads the finished numbers and writes about them, and it is told never to change or invent a figure.

Step one

Reads the report

Import one or more annual report PDFs or Excel (.xlsx) files. The app pulls out the statement tables, merges the years, shows how confident it is in each row, and runs checks so you can fix anything before you continue.

Step two

Analyses the statements

All ratios for one financial year, plus what changed once you add a second year. If a figure is missing, the ratio shows n/a. Nothing is estimated.

Step three

Values the company

The DCF model starts every assumption from your own history. Change any of them on the Assumptions tab and the value per share, WACC and sensitivity grids recalculate. Three or more years of history works best.

The DCF model

Pick DCF Model when you start a project. After you confirm the statements, the app fills in a full set of forecast assumptions from your own history. You can change any of them. Nothing is hidden: every number in the valuation is calculated from the statements and the assumptions you can see.

How the valuation is built

  • Free cash flow to firm for five years: EBIT less tax, plus depreciation, less capex, less the change in working capital.
  • WACC from the capital asset pricing model: risk-free rate, expected market return and beta give the cost of equity. Cost of debt is the risk-free rate plus a spread. The weights come from market value and borrowings.
  • Terminal value with a constant growth rate, discounted back with the fifth-year factor.
  • Bridge to equity: enterprise value, plus cash and investments (with an optional haircut), less borrowings, divided by shares outstanding.
  • Sensitivity: value per share for WACC against terminal growth, and WACC against terminal EBIT margin.
  • Assumption checks flag the usual mistakes: WACC too close to terminal growth, terminal capex below depreciation, an unrealistic reinvestment rate, or a terminal margin above the best year in your history.
Add the price and share count

Value per share and upside need a share price and the number of shares on the Assumptions tab. Without them the app still shows enterprise and equity value, and WACC treats the company as all equity.

Get a free Gemini API key

An API key is a long password that lets the app talk to Gemini on your behalf. Getting one takes about two minutes and needs only a Google account.

  1. Open Google AI Studio

    Go to aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with your Google account. Accept the terms if asked.

  2. Click "Create API key"

    Use "Get API key" in the left sidebar if you land elsewhere. Choose an existing Google Cloud project or let AI Studio create one for you.

  3. Copy the key

    It starts with AIza. Keep it private. Do not post it in screenshots, chats or public code.

  4. Open the app: Settings → AI & API key

    Set Provider to Google Gemini (AI Studio), paste the key, save, then press Test connection.

  5. Choose Advanced AI

    In the AI picker, select Advanced AI and tick "Remember my AI choice" if you want it kept. Then press Generate report on any analysed company.

Free tier limits

The free tier is rate limited (requests per minute and per day) and Google changes the numbers and the models it covers from time to time. Check the usage page in AI Studio for your current limits. In our own testing one Gemini model allowed only 20 free requests a day. Every critique, report and follow-up question uses one request. If the app says "rate limit or free quota reached", wait a minute and try again.

Privacy on the free tier

Google may use prompts and responses on the free tier to improve its products. Do not send confidential or client statements through a free key. Linking billing moves you to the paid tier, which has different data terms. Read Google's current terms before relying on this.

The key is stored only on your device and is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com. You can remove it any time from the same Settings screen. If you think a key leaked, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in is optional. Projects stay on your device.

Does the AI change my numbers?

No. The app calculates every figure. The AI is given the finished numbers and told to quote them exactly and not recalculate.

Which file types can I import?

PDF and Excel (.xlsx or .xlsm). You can add several files at once and the app merges their years. For Excel, put each statement on its own sheet, or under its own heading, with a row of years above the numbers. Exports from Screener.in are recognised. Scanned PDFs without selectable text are not read. Always review the rows before confirming.

Can I download the results?

Yes. Use Download Excel for a workbook with live formulas or Download PDF for a printable summary.

Where do the DCF assumptions come from?

They start from your own statements: recent growth, margins, tax rate, D&A, capex and working capital. The risk-free rate, market return and beta are estimates. Review each one on the Assumptions tab before you rely on the value.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. This build is Android only.

Can I use it without any AI?

Yes. Import, review, the statement analysis, the DCF and the Excel and PDF downloads all work without a key. Only the AI critique, the written report and follow-up questions need one.
