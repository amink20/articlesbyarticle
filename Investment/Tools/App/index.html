What the app does

Financial Analyst turns an annual report into numbers you can check. The app does all the calculating. The AI only reads the finished numbers and writes about them, and it is told never to change or invent a figure.

Step one

Reads the report

Import an annual report as a PDF. The app pulls out the statement tables, shows how confident it is in each row, and runs checks so you can fix anything before you continue. You can also type the figures in by hand.

Step two

Analyses the statements

All ratios for one financial year, plus what changed once you add a second year. If a figure is missing, the ratio shows n/a. Nothing is estimated.

Step three

Builds a DCF

Pick historical data, set forecast assumptions under separate scenarios, and see the valuation bridge and a sensitivity grid. Three or more years of history works best.

The flow

ImportDrop the annual report PDF, or enter figures manually.
ReviewCheck extracted rows, confidence and the app's own checks, then confirm.
AnalyseStatement analysis and company analysis (DCF) are independent models.
ReportYour chosen AI writes a final report from the confirmed numbers.

Good to know

  • Projects are saved on your phone. Signing in is optional.
  • Adding a listed company's price and share count unlocks a Share Details tab.
  • Light and dark themes are included.
  • Statement analysis and DCF are separate: the valuation never depends on the statement analysis.

Get a free Gemini API key

An API key is a long password that lets the app talk to Gemini on your behalf. Getting one takes about two minutes and needs only a Google account.

  1. Open Google AI Studio

    Go to aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with your Google account. Accept the terms if asked.

  2. Click "Create API key"

    Use "Get API key" in the left sidebar if you land elsewhere. Choose an existing Google Cloud project or let AI Studio create one for you.

  3. Copy the key

    It starts with AIza. Keep it private. Do not post it in screenshots, chats or public code.

  4. Open the app: Settings → AI & API key

    Set Provider to Google Gemini (AI Studio), paste the key, save, then press Test connection.

  5. Choose Advanced AI

    In the AI picker, select Advanced AI and tick "Remember my AI choice" if you want it kept. Then press Generate report on any analysed company.

Free tier limits

The free tier is rate limited (requests per minute and per day) and Google changes the numbers and the models it covers from time to time. Check the usage page in AI Studio for your current limits. If the app says "rate limit or free quota reached", wait a minute and try again.

Privacy on the free tier

Google may use prompts and responses on the free tier to improve its products. Do not send confidential or client statements through a free key. Linking billing moves you to the paid tier, which has different data terms. Read Google's current terms before relying on this.

The key is stored only on your device and is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com. You can remove it any time from the same Settings screen. If you think a key leaked, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in is optional. Projects stay on your device.

Does the AI change my numbers?

No. The app calculates every figure. The AI is given the finished numbers and told to quote them exactly and not recalculate.

Which file types can I import?

PDF only for now. Scanned PDFs without selectable text may extract poorly, so always review the rows before confirming.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. This build is Android only.

Can I use it without any AI?

Yes. Import, review and the statement and DCF screens work without a key. Only the written report needs an AI.
