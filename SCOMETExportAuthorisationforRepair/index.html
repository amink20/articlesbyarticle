SCOMET Export Authorisation for Repair: Procedure, Eligibility & Post-Export Compliance

27 July 2025· 10 min read· 📄 ArticlesByArticle
SA
Sachin Agrawal
Executive, Taxcraft Advisors LLP

India's SCOMET (Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies) framework governs the export of dual-use items and technologies that carry strategic and national security significance. Where a SCOMET item previously imported into India requires repair or replacement abroad, the exporter must obtain a specific export authorisation from the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) before initiating the shipment. This article provides a practical guide to the procedure, eligibility conditions, documentation requirements, and post-export compliance obligations applicable to such transactions.

Given the sensitivity of SCOMET items and the regulatory scrutiny they attract, businesses and their compliance teams must approach this process with precision. A misstep — whether in documentation, timelines, or re-import obligations — can attract significant penal consequences under the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992 and allied legislation.

What is SCOMET and Why Does It Matter?

SCOMET is India's export control list, maintained by the DGFT under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It identifies items — including chemicals, micro-organisms, materials, equipment, and technologies — that are subject to export controls due to their potential for dual use in civilian and military applications. The list is broadly aligned with international non-proliferation regimes such as the Wassenaar Arrangement, the Australia Group, the Missile Technology Control Regime (MTCR), and the Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG).

Any export of a SCOMET item, including re-export for repair or warranty replacement, requires prior authorisation from the DGFT. The absence of such authorisation — even where the item was originally imported and is merely being returned for repair — constitutes an unauthorised export and attracts strict penalties under applicable law.

Filing the Application on the DGFT Portal

The application for SCOMET export authorisation must be filed online through the DGFT portal. Applicants should navigate to: Services >> Export Management System >> License for SCOMET Exports >> Apply for Export Authorization of SCOMET Item >> Apply for New Authorization.

Mandatory FeeA non-refundable application fee of INR 1,000/- is payable at the time of filing the application on the DGFT portal.

Documents Required for the Application

The following documents must be uploaded on the DGFT portal along with the application:

  • Technical specifications or product datasheet of the SCOMET item
  • Purchase Order, Service Agreement, Master Service Agreement (MSA), or Contract
  • Proof of original import — Bill of Entry with item description and quantity highlighted
  • Warranty policy or conditions governing repair or replacement of defective or damaged items
  • Contractual documents such as Purchase Order or Agreement with the OEM or importer
  • Legal Undertaking on ₹200 stamp paper in the format prescribed under Appendix 10K of the Foreign Trade Policy

Where the transaction involves a foreign subsidiary of an Indian parent company, or another subsidiary of a foreign parent company, an End User Certificate (EUC) in the format prescribed under Appendix 2S(iv) of the Foreign Trade Policy may also be required as part of the documentation set under Appendix 10D.

Eligibility & Conditions for Export Authorisation

Key Eligibility ConditionsThe SCOMET item must have been originally imported into India and is now being exported solely for repair or replacement due to defects. No change in the original specifications or characteristics is permitted. The export must be made to the same entity from which the item was imported, or to the OEM. The export must be consistent with the original import conditions or contractual terms.

Additionally, authorisation will not be granted where the original export authorisation issued by the exporting country has been suspended, modified, or revoked; or where the destination country is under UNSC sanctions or has been assessed as high-risk by India's Inter-Ministerial Working Group (IMWG). No separate End Use Certificate is required where the export is solely for repair.

Post-Export & Re-Import Compliance

90-Day Re-Import DeadlineThe repaired or replaced item must be brought back to India within 90 days of export, or within such extended time as may be permitted by the DGFT. If additional time is required, prior approval must be sought from the DGFT with adequate justification before the deadline expires.

Where the item cannot be re-imported — for instance, where it is found to be beyond economical repair — evidence of its destruction abroad must be submitted to the DGFT within 90 days of the export. Upon successful re-import, the Bill of Entry must be submitted to the DGFT in the format prescribed under Annexure-I of Appendix 10K.

Practical Considerations for Compliance Teams

Businesses dealing in dual-use items should build robust internal processes around export authorisation management — maintaining a live tracker of all active SCOMET authorisations and their re-import deadlines, ensuring that warranty and repair arrangements with foreign OEMs are documented in writing before shipment, and training logistics and finance teams to flag SCOMET items at the point of export.

Conclusion

The SCOMET authorisation framework for repair exports reflects India's commitment to international export control norms while providing a structured pathway for legitimate warranty and repair transactions. Businesses must treat this not as a one-time filing exercise, but as a continuous compliance obligation — from application through to re-import and post-export reporting.

Key TakeawaySCOMET export authorisation for repair is not merely a procedural formality — it is a legally mandated compliance obligation with defined timelines, strict eligibility conditions, and post-export reporting requirements that must be fulfilled without exception.
